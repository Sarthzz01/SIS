Mutual Funds: Grow Your Wealth with Smart Investments

Diversified. Professionally Managed. Built for Long-Term Compounding.

At Sukhmira Investment Services LLP, we help investors build sustainable wealth through carefully selected mutual fund solutions. A mutual fund pools money from multiple investors and invests across equities, fixed income securities, or hybrid instruments under professional fund management.

  • Equity Mutual Funds: Invest in high-conviction stocks across Large Cap, Mid Cap, and Flexi Cap categories for long-term capital growth.
  • Debt Mutual Funds: Focus on fixed-income instruments like government securities and high-quality corporate debt for capital preservation and stable returns.
  • Tax-Saving Funds (ELSS): Build wealth while claiming tax deductions of up to ₹1.5 Lakhs under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act with the shortest lock-in period (3 years).
  • Hybrid Mutual Funds: Dynamically blend equities and fixed income to balance growth with downside protection.

Bonds: Stable Returns. Lower Risk. Long-Term Wealth.

Reliable regular income while preserving your core capital.

A bond is an investment instrument where you lend capital to a government entity, municipality, or corporation. In return, the issuer pays you scheduled interest payments and returns the principal at maturity. Bonds are ideal for investors who prefer consistent, predictable returns over equity swings.

  • Government & Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB): Backed by the Government of India, offering the highest degree of safety and tax-free capital gains at maturity.
  • Corporate Bonds & NCDs: Issued by top-rated Indian corporations and financial institutions offering attractive yields higher than standard savings.
  • Tax-Free Bonds: High-value instruments issued by public sector undertakings (PSUs) where interest earned is 100% tax-free under Section 10(15)(iv)(h).

Fixed Deposits: Guaranteed Returns. Assured Peace of Mind.

Secure lump-sum investments with zero equity volatility and flexible interest payouts.

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is a trusted investment avenue where you deposit money for a designated tenure and earn guaranteed interest. At Sukhmira Investment Services LLP, we help clients choose between leading Scheduled Commercial Banks and AAA-rated Corporate FDs that offer higher interest rates.

  • Corporate & NBFC Fixed Deposits: Earn competitive interest rates (up to 7.80% – 8.50%+ p.a.) with CRISIL/ICRA AAA and AA+ rated safety.
  • Senior Citizen Privilege (+0.50% Extra): Additional 0.25% to 0.50% higher interest rate specifically designed for senior citizens.
  • Flexible Payout Frequencies: Choose monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or cumulative compounding payout options to match your retirement cash flow needs.
  • Tax-Saving FDs: 5-year lock-in bank FDs eligible for tax deduction under Section 80C.

54 EC Bonds: Save Capital Gains Tax on Property Sale
